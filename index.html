One guide, eight looks

同一个产品，八种长相

A prototype of an English restaurant guide for first-time visitors to Beijing. All eight have the same content and the same steps: describe what you want, pick a place, build an order, show a card to staff. Only the design differs.

这是一个给第一次来北京的外国游客用的英文找餐厅原型。八版内容和步骤完全一样（一句话找店、看店、帮我点单、给店员看的卡），只有设计不同。

  1. 01Gazette插画杂志›White page, black rules, flat illustration.
  2. 02Night Market极繁›Dark, loud, big type, stickers and tickets.
  3. 03Plain极简›Black and white, one typeface, no pictures.
  4. 04After Dark深色›Dark ground, serif type, one place per screen.
  5. 05Lookbook时尚杂志›Huge headlines, off-centre layout, sideways pages.
  6. 06Deck滑卡片›Swipe cards, a bottom sheet, a floating bar.
  7. 07Wayfinding指示牌›Airport signage: high contrast, pictograms, numbered steps.
  8. 08Soft圆润›Warm colour blocks, round corners, sticker drawings.

In each one, try to get to the card you would show to staff, then come back. Three questions: could you tell what this is within five seconds? Would you trust it in a city you don't know? Which number would you actually use?

每一版都试着点到「给店员看的卡」再回来。三个问题：5 秒内看得出这是什么吗？在陌生城市你会信它吗？你真的会用的是几号？

Design test only. The restaurant details are drafts that have not been checked; please do not rely on them. The search and ordering helpers are scripted and no AI model is connected.

这只是设计测试。餐厅信息是未核实的草稿，请不要当真使用；找店和点单是写好的规则，没有接 AI 模型。
